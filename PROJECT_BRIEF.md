# Project Brief (PROJECT_BRIEF.md)

## What TwinStack is
TwinStack is a platform designed to bridge the gap between vision and execution. It provides a structured environment where an Architect structures a product into actionable requests, a Builder executes those requirements, and the community can observe the build process transparently.

## Who created it
TwinStack was created by **Parallax Studio**.

## Why it exists
TwinStack exists to fulfill the philosophy that **clarity is speed**. By strictly separating the architectural structure from the execution layer, TwinStack eliminates middle-manager overhead and focuses entirely on high-fidelity shipping. 

## User roles
- **The Architect**: Product structure, specs, strategy.
- **The Builder**: Implementation, engineering, performance.
- **Vibecoder Guest**: Community observer, learning, forking.
- **Subscribed User**: Paying customer who manages private workspaces while observing TwinStack.

## Main workflows
1. **Architectual Spceification**: The Architect creates and approves requests (technical and non-technical descriptions).
2. **Implementation**: The Builder picks up the requests from the queue and implements them.
3. **Build-in-Public**: Guests and Subscribed Users securely observe the TwinStack development lifecycle in read-only mode.

## Build-in-public purpose
TwinStack serves as Parallax Studio's official "Build-in-Public" project. It operates transparently to build community trust and showcase the execution velocity of the Architect/Builder model.

## Proof of Mind concept
TwinStack embodies "Proof of Mind" by making the thought process behind product engineering visible: visitors can read the exact architectural instructions before seeing the executed codebase.
